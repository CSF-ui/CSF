Celebrating 20 Years of God's Faithfulness

For two decades, CSF Church has stood as a beacon of light, grace, and community love here in Bulacan. We invite you to look back at how God's hand has guided every step of our journey.

Our Journey

Started with a handful of believers meeting in a living room, growing into a vibrant spiritual home for hundreds of families today.

Our Mission

To glorify God by making disciples through authentic worship, passionate prayer, and compassionate community outreach.

Our Vision

To build a flourishing, multi-generational church that transforms cities and nations through the power of the Gospel.
